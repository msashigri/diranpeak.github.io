Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions for Fleet Partners

Learn more about our standards, booking process, and how we support our drivers.

What requirements must vehicles meet to drive to Askole or K2 Base Camp trailhead? +
Vehicles traveling to Askole must have operational 4-wheel drive with low-range transfer gears, high ground clearance, heavy-duty suspension, all-terrain tires with adequate tread, and a functional spare tire and emergency tool kit.
Are drivers required with vehicle rental? +
Yes. Due to the complex terrain and river crossings in Gilgit-Baltistan, all Diran Peak Adventure partner vehicles are chartered with verified, licensed local drivers who possess years of experience navigating the Karakoram Highway and mountain switchbacks.
How and when do vehicle owners get paid? +
We provide a 50% fuel and operational advance prior to trip departure from Skardu or Islamabad, and the remaining balance is settled within 2 business days of trip conclusion via direct bank transfer, JazzCash, or cash disbursement at our Skardu office.
Can an individual owner with only 1 vehicle apply? +
Absolutely. We contract both individual owner-operators with a single Prado or Hilux as well as large transport fleet companies managing dozens of coasters and vans.

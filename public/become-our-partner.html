Frequently Asked Questions

What are the key criteria for transport partners? ▼
Vehicles traveling mountain routes must have functional 4-wheel drive, high ground clearance, regular maintenance logs, valid road taxes and fitness certificates, spare tires, jack tools, and experienced drivers familiar with mountain driving conditions.
How and when do partners receive payments? ▼
For transport and hotels, we disburse a 50% advance before the tour begins. The remaining balance is paid within 48 hours of trip completion via direct bank wire transfer, JazzCash, or cash collection at our Skardu office.
Can individual drivers with just one vehicle partner with us? ▼
Yes! We contract individual owner-operators as well as large transport fleets with multiple Land Cruisers, Hilux jeeps, and Coaster buses.
What support is provided for international B2B agencies? ▼
We provide full ground handling including official government Letter of Invitation (LOI) for Pakistan mountaineering and trekking visas, military liaison officer coordination, domestic air booking, private charter vehicles, and licensed high-altitude sirdars.

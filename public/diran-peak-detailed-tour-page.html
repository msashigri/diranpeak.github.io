Expedition Overview

The K2 Base Camp Trek is globally revered as the world's premier wilderness expedition. Starting from the legendary gateway of Askole, hikers traverse the colossal 62-kilometer Baltoro Glacier to arrive at Concordia — the "Throne Room of the Mountain Gods" — where four 8,000-meter giants (K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I, and Gasherbrum II) tower simultaneously above the ice.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

01 Day 1: Arrival in Islamabad - Expedition Briefing
▼
Arrive at Islamabad International Airport. Our logistics team receives you and transfers you to our partner hotel. Official briefing at the Alpine Club of Pakistan regarding permits and government liaison regulations.
Altitude: 540 m Accommodation: Islamabad Hotel
02 Day 2: Scenic Mountain Flight to Skardu (2,230m)
▼
Take the breathtaking PIA flight along Nanga Parbat to Skardu airport. Meet with your senior Balti mountain guide and review high-altitude gear and provisions.
Altitude: 2,230 m Accommodation: Skardu Resort
03 Day 3: 4x4 Jeep Drive to Askole (3,000m)
▼
Scenic 4x4 Jeep safari following the roaring Braldu River gorge into the last inhabited Balti village of Askole. First night under alpine tents.
Altitude: 3,000 m Accommodation: Askole Campsite
04 Day 4: Trek from Askole to Jhola & Paiju (3,450m)
▼
Enter Central Karakoram National Park. Trek past the Biafo Glacier snout towards Paiju campsite beneath the iconic granite spires of Paiju Peak.
Altitude: 3,450 m Trek Time: 6-7 Hours
05 Day 5: Urdukas & Goro II - The Glacier Highway
▼
Step onto the legendary Baltoro Glacier. Trek past Trango Towers, Cathedral Peak, and Lobsang Spires towards Goro II camp situated directly upon the moraine.
Altitude: 4,300 m Trek Time: 6 Hours
06 Day 6: Concordia - Throne Room of the Mountain Gods
▼
Trek into Concordia (4,691m), the confluence of the Baltoro and Godwin-Austen Glaciers. Spectacular 360-degree amphitheater of K2, Broad Peak, Mitre Peak, and Gasherbrum IV.
Altitude: 4,691 m Accommodation: Concordia Ice Camp
07 Day 7: K2 Base Camp (5,150m) & Gilkey Memorial
▼
Dawn push towards K2 Base Camp directly beneath the Savage Mountain's South Face. Pay respects at the Art Gilkey Memorial before returning to Concordia.
Altitude: 5,150 m Highlight: Standing at foot of K2
